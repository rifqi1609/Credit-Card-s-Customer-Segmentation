## 📌 Problem Background
Understanding our customers is a must in today's crowded market. Maintaining and improving customer relations can lengthen their lifetime value with higher quality transactions. This project focuses on the **segmentation of credit card holders** based on their behavioral patterns and distinct characteristics.

## 🎯 Project Output
The objective of this clustering is to identify the most effective, personalized strategies for increasing credit card usage frequency across different consumer segments.
